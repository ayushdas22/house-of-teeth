What We Do

Complete dental care
under one roof

From your first check-up to your dream smile, we offer the full spectrum of modern dentistry.

🔍
Examination & Cleaning

Thorough check-ups, X-rays, and professional scaling to keep your teeth healthy and bright.

🔧
Fillings & Restorations

Tooth-coloured composite fillings that restore strength while looking completely natural.

✂️
Extractions

Painless simple and surgical extractions, including wisdom tooth removal, with expert post-care.

✨
Teeth Whitening

In-clinic and take-home whitening treatments for a noticeably brighter smile in one session.

💎
Veneers & Smile Makeover

Porcelain veneers, bonding, and full-smile redesign for the confidence you deserve.

📐
Braces & Clear Aligners

Metal braces, ceramic braces, and removable clear aligners to straighten teeth at any age.

🛡️
Root Canal Treatment

Pain-free RCT using rotary instruments — save your natural tooth instead of extracting it.

👑
Crowns & Bridges

Zirconia and ceramic crowns that look, feel, and function just like your real teeth.

🦴
Dental Implants

Permanent titanium implants that replace missing teeth with a natural look and lifetime durability.
